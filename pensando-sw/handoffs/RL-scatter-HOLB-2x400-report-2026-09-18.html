Findings

  • RL recovers 2-port HOLB to line rate on the scatter build — core validation holds at 2×400G.
  • no-RCN path-1: RL-off 1327/1391 (q7/q9) → RL-on 1530 = +15% / +10%, full line rate. Recovery fades as QP grows and the port split evens (q63+ = no HOLB).
  • RCN ω7 path-1: HOLB is milder (RCN self-heals to ~1490) so RL adds a smaller boost (q9 1380→1401, q33 1487→1507).
  • path-4 (2 paths/port, non-flat branch): self-balances at line rate, RL idle — no HOLB.
  • unidir: low-QP path-1 is demand-starved (q7 675 < 777) — a floor RL can't raise, not HOLB. Higher QP reaches line rate.
  • RL cost at line rate: bidir ~0% (1518=1518), uni ~0.6% (772 vs 777). RL config 399.5G/300K/win4 (user-tuned).
